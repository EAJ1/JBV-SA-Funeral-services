Find the Right Insurance

Choosing the right funeral insurance can be overwhelming. Our experts help you navigate options to find coverage that fits your needs and budget. We offer comprehensive funeral insurance policies designed to protect your family from the financial burden of funeral expenses.

Why Choose Funeral Insurance?

  • Pre-paid funeral expenses with guaranteed coverage
  • Protection against inflation and rising costs
  • Peace of mind for your family during difficult times
  • Flexible payment options with affordable premiums
  • Tax-free lump sum payout upon death
  • No medical exams required for most policies

Types of Funeral Insurance

Term Life Insurance

Provides coverage for a specific period. Premiums are lower but coverage ends if you outlive the term.

  • Lower monthly premiums
  • Coverage for 10-30 years
  • Convert to permanent insurance

Whole Life Insurance

Lifetime coverage with guaranteed premiums. Builds cash value over time.

  • Lifetime coverage
  • Guaranteed premiums
  • Cash value accumulation
  • Loan against policy value

Funeral Assurance

Specifically designed for funeral expenses with simplified underwriting.

  • Guaranteed acceptance (no medical exam)
  • Immediate coverage available
  • Fixed premiums
  • Direct payout to funeral provider

Key Considerations

  • Coverage Amount: R50,000 - R500,000 depending on needs
  • Age and Health: Premiums increase with age; health conditions may affect eligibility
  • Waiting Periods: 6-24 months for natural causes in some policies
  • Beneficiaries: Choose who receives the payout
  • Riders: Additional coverage options available
